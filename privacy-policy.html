01

Scope & Governing Principle

This Privacy & Policy establishes the operating principles under which Luxon Stone India receives, reviews, stores, processes and protects information provided through its website, enquiry forms, B2B procurement discussions, consultation workflows and related commercial communications.

Our operating principle is direct: information supplied for a commercial relationship remains controlled information. Access is limited to personnel and enterprise functions that require it for legitimate business execution, compliance or service delivery.

02

Enterprise Confidentiality & IP Security

All project specifications, floor plans, CAD files, drawings, architectural references, quantities, schedules and RERA data submitted through our forms or during B2B engagement are treated as confidential commercial material and protected under strict non-disclosure principles.

Luxon Stone India treats architectural blueprints as proprietary intelligence. Such material is not considered ordinary marketing information merely because it is submitted through a web form, email, consultation or procurement workflow.

Controlled handling

  • Project information is accessed only for legitimate evaluation, estimation, production planning, procurement, order logistics, site coordination, documentation, account management or related enterprise functions.
  • Internal handling is performed on controlled enterprise systems and through authorized business channels.
  • Architectural or commercial material is not intentionally repurposed for unrelated marketing, competitive intelligence, publication or public distribution.
  • Where a separate NDA, confidentiality agreement or enterprise contract applies, that agreement remains controlling to the extent of any conflict.
03

Data Usage & Business Purpose

Client and prospect information is processed strictly for legitimate B2B purposes, including enquiry response, quotation preparation, procurement, order management, production coordination, freight planning, site logistics, account servicing, invoicing, recordkeeping and legal or regulatory obligations.

Luxon Stone India does not sell, lease or monetize client information for data-broker activity. Information is not knowingly provided to third-party data brokers for resale, profiling or unrelated commercial exploitation.

Commercial communications

Where a prospect requests a consultation, quote, sample, site measurement or commercial follow-up, relevant contact information may be used to communicate about that request. Clients may request that non-essential commercial communications be stopped.

04

Non-Disclosure & Limited External Access

Luxon Stone India maintains a strict need-to-know approach. Information may be disclosed only where reasonably required to perform the requested service, fulfill an order, comply with law, protect legitimate business interests, or administer an agreed enterprise relationship.

Where external service providers or operational partners are legitimately required, access should be limited to the minimum information necessary for the stated purpose and handled subject to appropriate confidentiality and security expectations.

We do not authorize the sale, lease or deliberate transfer of client data to third-party data brokers.

05

Freight Subsidy & Promotional Offer Terms

Promotional logistics support is a limited commercial allocation and is not a standing entitlement. Eligibility may depend on enterprise status, project scope, geography, order structure, commercial viability and the specific written offer issued by Luxon Stone India.

Commercial control

“Complimentary Site Freight / Free Delivery offers are strictly limited promotional allocations (capped at a maximum of 10 portals per qualifying B2B enterprise trial order). Extended freight subsidies beyond this cap require formal evaluation.”

Any freight subsidy, complimentary site delivery or promotional logistics commitment applies only to the scope expressly stated in the relevant commercial communication, quotation, order acknowledgement or agreement. No promotional statement should be interpreted as creating an unlimited or perpetual freight obligation.

06

Retention, Security & Operational Controls

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the commercial purpose for which it was collected, satisfy contractual commitments, maintain accurate business records, address disputes, or meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Reasonable administrative, technical and organizational controls should be used to reduce unauthorized access, alteration, misuse or disclosure. No internet-based system can be represented as absolutely risk-free; however, Luxon Stone India’s stated policy is to apply controlled-access practices appropriate to the commercial sensitivity of the information handled.

07

Client Requests & Policy Enquiries

Clients and authorized representatives may contact Luxon Stone India regarding information submitted through the website, a project enquiry, procurement engagement or commercial relationship. Requests should identify the business or project context sufficiently for the relevant record to be located and reviewed.

Where a request concerns confidentiality, an enterprise NDA, contractual data handling, a legal issue, or a non-standard commercial arrangement, the matter may be escalated for executive or legal review.
